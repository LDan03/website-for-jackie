The stay

Stay in the centre of Kirkwall, minutes from a town centre steeped in history — visit St Magnus Cathedral or the Earl's Palace, then take a short stroll to the harbour where you'll be spoilt for choice in restaurants, pubs, hotels and sights.

The Old Printworks has seven matching en-suite bedrooms with a shared kitchen, sitting room and laundry facilities — comfortable for a weekend, and priced to make sense for a month. All amenities are close by, including supermarkets and takeaways, and the bus station is minutes away.

Staying longer? Discounted rates apply to longer stays — ideal for contractors, visiting workers and slow travellers. Enquire for a tailored quote.

Location

The Old Printworks sits in the centre of Kirkwall, Orkney's capital — a harbour town steeped in Norse history. Step out of the door and you're minutes from the shops, the cathedral and the waterfront.

Arriving by ferry or bus? The bus station is a few minutes' walk, with connections across the Orkney Mainland and to the ferry terminals.

  • ~3 min Town centre, shops & takeaways
  • ~5 min St Magnus Cathedral & the Earl's Palace
  • ~5 min Bus station
